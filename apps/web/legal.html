Biruni™: terms of use & licence

Version 2026-10. Plain language first. This is a template written for a student prototype, not legal advice: have a lawyer review it before commercial use.

1. Biruni helps; you decide

Biruni is a software assistant. It suggests options, can book or pay within the limits you set (for example ₹2,000 per disruption), translates, negotiates and alerts people you choose. You are responsible for your decisions, your travel, your bookings and payments, your safety and the safety of the people travelling with you. Biruni is a tool for help, not the final authority.

2. Check before you rely on it

Prices, schedules, availability, maps, translations and AI-generated answers can be wrong or out of date. Some features are simulated until real partner accounts are connected (for example booking partners, telephony, payments, bank data); the app labels them as simulated. Confirm important details with the operator, hotel or authority directly.

3. Emergencies

In an emergency in India, call 112. Biruni's SOS sends alerts to people you trust and other Biruni users who chose to help. It does not contact police, ambulance or rescue services on its own and cannot guarantee anyone will respond. Location may be inaccurate, missing or stale.

4. Your data

Your data is encrypted with a key unlocked by your PIN. If you forget your PIN, your data cannot be recovered. Shared-trip messages and SOS details are encrypted to their recipients; the server can see who is in a shared trip and who alerted whom, and when, but not what was said or where anyone is. API keys you enter for AI models are stored encrypted in your space. Third-party services you connect (AI providers, maps, calendars, telephony) receive the data needed for the request and are governed by their own terms.

5. Automatic actions

When autopilot is on, Biruni may act without asking within your limits (book a replacement, pay within the authority), and gives you a 30-second undo. Turn autopilot off at any time. Biruni never touches money you marked as committed (rent, EMI, fees) and never sells investments.
